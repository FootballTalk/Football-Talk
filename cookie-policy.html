Cookie Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how FootballTalk.uk uses cookies, browser storage and similar technologies. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Websites can also use similar browser technologies such as local storage. These technologies can remember preferences, support security and site functions, measure performance and, where permitted, support advertising.

How Football Talk uses them

CategoryPurpose
EssentialRequired for core site operation, security, network delivery and functions that a visitor requests. These technologies do not require advertising consent where the law permits them as strictly necessary.
PreferencesMay remember settings such as football-club or display preferences so the site can provide a more useful experience.
MeasurementWhere enabled and permitted, analytics or performance tools may help Football Talk understand how pages and features are used and diagnose technical problems.
AdvertisingWhere Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and protect advertising, subject to the visitor's consent choices and applicable law.

Google AdSense and consent

Football Talk is applying to use Google AdSense. For visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Football Talk will use a Google-certified consent management platform where required before personalised AdSense advertising is served. The consent message will allow eligible visitors to make the choices required by the advertising platform and applicable rules.

Third-party services

Some pages link to or use services operated by third parties, including hosting, email delivery, social platforms, video services and commercial partners. A third party may set or read its own cookies when you interact with its service. Those cookies are governed by that provider's policies.

Your choices

Where consent is required, you can accept or refuse the relevant non-essential purposes through the consent controls presented on the site. You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect parts of the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when site features, advertising or service providers change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.
