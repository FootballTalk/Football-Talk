Email & Privacy

When you join Football Talk Weekly, we use the email address you provide to send Football Talk news, community updates, competitions and occasional partner content that you have consented to receive.

You can withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or the unsubscribe page on FootballTalk.uk. Withdrawing consent does not affect your use of the Football Talk website or Members Area.
