Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains how Football Talk handles personal information when you use FootballTalk.uk, subscribe to Football Talk Weekly, contact us or use features that store preferences.

Information you provide

If you subscribe to Football Talk Weekly, we process the email address you provide together with information needed to record and manage your consent, such as signup source, date and time. If you contact us, we process the information contained in your message so we can respond.

Website data and cookies

The website and services used to operate it may process technical information such as device/browser information, IP-derived information, page requests and basic usage data for security, reliability, analytics and service operation. Browser storage may also be used for site preferences such as selected football-club settings.

Advertising and Google services

Football Talk may use Google services, including Google AdSense if and when advertising is approved and enabled. Advertising and analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements. Where consent is legally required, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism before non-essential advertising or measurement technologies are used.

Email

Football Talk Weekly is sent only to subscribers who have provided the required consent. Emails can include Football Talk news, community updates, competitions and occasional partner content. You can unsubscribe using the link provided in an email or the site's unsubscribe page.

How we use information

We use personal information to provide requested services, operate and secure the website, manage subscriptions and preferences, respond to enquiries, understand site performance and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your email address.

Service providers

We use specialist service providers to host and operate Football Talk and its communications. They may process information on our behalf only as required to provide those services. External websites linked from Football Talk have their own privacy practices.

Retention and your rights

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including legal and record-keeping requirements. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@footballtalk.uk. You can also use our contact page.
