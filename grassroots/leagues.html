How it works

Start with the league. Build with the clubs.

1. Share your source

Send the public league page and, if you have them, direct fixtures and table links.

2. Agree the coverage

We’ll confirm the divisions, data source, attribution and what your league would like us to feature.

3. Invite clubs

Once the league area is ready, clubs can add approved news, match reports and short interviews over time.

If your league uses FA Full-Time, an administrator can find official table, recent results and upcoming fixtures embed codes in Full-Time Admin → Media → Code Snippets. We can discuss those after your initial enquiry. Please do not send login details. A public link lets us review your league, but we’ll agree an approved display method before reproducing live data.
