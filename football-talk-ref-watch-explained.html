Why Football Talk created Ref Watch

VAR arguments move quickly and often lose the detail. Ref Watch was built to slow the big decisions down and separate fact, professional interpretation and opinion.

By Mark Dauncey, Football Talk · Published 21 September 2026 · Football Talk analysis

By Monday morning, one refereeing decision can have been clipped, reposted and argued about thousands of times. The original incident is often buried beneath reactions. Football Talk created Ref Watch to give those debates a repeatable structure.

1. Establish the incident

We begin with what happened on the pitch: the decision, the relevant phase of play and what VAR or the referee did. This section is intended to be descriptive rather than a verdict.

2. Attribute the professional view

Where professional referee analysis or an official explanation is available, we summarise it and identify the source. We do not present somebody else’s analysis as Football Talk’s own and we link readers to source material where appropriate.

3. Give the Football Talk verdict

This is where our own editorial judgement belongs. Agree, disagree or call it genuinely debatable — but explain why. A label without reasoning adds little; the point is to show the argument that leads to the verdict.

The important distinction: reporting what an official body or analyst said is not the same thing as Football Talk agreeing with it.

4. Hand it to the supporters

Football is not improved by pretending every judgement call has one universally accepted interpretation. The final stage is the fan question. Supporters can agree with our reasoning, challenge it or raise the part of the incident they think everybody has missed.

Why this adds more than an embedded clip

A video can show an incident, but a useful football publication has to add context. Ref Watch is therefore built around original summaries, attribution and Football Talk commentary. External footage or analysis, when linked or embedded lawfully, supports that work rather than becoming the whole page.
