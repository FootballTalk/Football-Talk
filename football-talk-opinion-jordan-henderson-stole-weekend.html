Jordan Henderson has already stolen the weekend

An outstanding performance by such a great player. Jordan Henderson has already made himself the story of the weekend.

By Mark Dauncey, Football Talk · Published 10 October 2026 · Opinion

Some performances make a match feel like a personal statement. Jordan Henderson scored twice as Chelsea beat Bournemouth 5–1, and his display has already set the tone for the football weekend.

A brace, and a performance to remember

Henderson’s early goal gave Chelsea a flying start. His second, late in the game, put the result beyond doubt and completed a remarkable afternoon in blue. Two goals tell part of the story; the energy and assurance behind the performance made it stand out.

It was the sort of display that reminds you what an outstanding player Henderson is. He brought quality to the decisive moments and helped turn a big win into a moment supporters will remember well beyond the final whistle.

Weekend talking point: Jordan Henderson has already stolen the weekend.

There is plenty of football still to come, but that is a high bar to clear. A brace in a 5–1 win, delivered with the confidence of a player enjoying his football, makes Henderson the standout story for now.
